SUNSET PRESS — bundled fonts

The display faces bundled under app/fonts/ are licensed under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:

- Anton (Anton-Regular.ttf) — a condensed display face, used for the
  poster wordmark.
- Bebas Neue (BebasNeue-Regular.ttf) — a condensed sans, used for the
  kicker, footer and panel labels.
